# AI Hunter — Core Feature Ideas

1. **Perception Score** — Track each user's accuracy over time as a personal stat. The score reflects how sharp their eye is at detecting AI-generated images, updating after every guess. Could include streaks, ratings (e.g. "Novice Eye" → "AI Detective"), and a leaderboard.

2. **Popularity Reveal** — After a user picks, show a breakdown of how everyone else voted (e.g. "78% of people chose this one"). Styled as an animated stat reveal — the moment of social feedback that makes it satisfying and shareable, similar to Wishbone's core loop.

3. **Sleek Minimalist UI** — Apple-inspired design language: clean typography, generous whitespace, smooth transitions. The two images are the hero of every screen — no clutter, no noise. Dark mode first. Interactions feel tactile and intentional.
