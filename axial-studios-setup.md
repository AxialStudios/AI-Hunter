# Axial Studios — LLC + Apple Developer Setup

## Step 1: Register Axial Studios LLC in Minnesota
- **Site:** mncis.courts.state.mn.us (Minnesota Secretary of State)
- **Fee:** $135 online filing
- **Timeline:** Usually same day to 1–3 business days
- **Annual renewal:** $25/year after the first year
- Save the Certificate of Organization PDF when it arrives

## Step 2: Get an EIN from the IRS (free, instant)
- **Site:** irs.gov → "Apply for an EIN Online"
- Do this the same day you file the LLC
- Takes ~10 minutes, free, no waiting

## Step 3: Apply for a DUNS Number
- **Site:** dnb.com → request a DUNS number
- Free option: ~30 business days
- Expedited option: ~5 business days (~$200–300)
- You'll need: "Axial Studios LLC", Minnesota address, EIN, phone
- Start this immediately after Step 1 — it runs in parallel

## Step 4: Apple Developer Organization Account
- **Site:** developer.apple.com → Enroll → Organization
- **Fee:** $99/year
- Needs DUNS number + legal entity name matching D&B exactly ("Axial Studios LLC")
- Apple review after submission: 2–5 additional business days

## Realistic Total Timeline
If you start Steps 1–3 today with expedited DUNS: **~2 weeks end-to-end**

## Notes
- Minnesota is the right call — simple, no reason to file in Delaware unless you're raising VC money
- App Store review per app (separate from account): ~24–48 hrs per submission
